# Money Mule Activity

**Definition:** A money mule is a person who transfers illegally acquired money on behalf of others, often unknowingly. Mule accounts are used to receive and forward funds, making it harder to trace proceeds of crime.

## Key Indicators
- Account receives large deposits from unknown third parties followed by rapid outbound transfers
- Account holder is a student, recent immigrant, or newly employed individual with activity inconsistent with profile
- Funds received and forwarded within 24–48 hours, with a small percentage retained as "commission"
- Multiple incoming transfers from different sources, consolidated and sent onward
- Account opened recently with minimal activity before sudden high-volume transactions

## Common Mule Patterns
1. **Direct Mule:** Victim recruited online (job scams, romance scams) to receive and forward funds
2. **Professional Mule:** Individual knowingly participates in money laundering for a fee
3. **Mule Herder Network:** Organized group managing multiple mule accounts across institutions
4. **Account Takeover Mule:** Compromised accounts used without the holder's knowledge

## Red Flags
- "Work from home" job advertisements asking employees to process payments
- Social media recruitment for "money transfer agent" positions
- Sudden change in account behavior from dormant to high-volume
- Funds received from known fraud victims or compromised accounts
- Immediate withdrawal or transfer of received funds

## Risk Assessment
- **High Risk:** Rapid pass-through of funds from multiple unknown sources with no legitimate explanation
- **Medium Risk:** Account behavior change with partial explanation (e.g., new freelance work)
- **Low Risk:** One-time receipt from known party with clear purpose
