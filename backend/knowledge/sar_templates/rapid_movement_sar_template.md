# SAR Template: Rapid Fund Movement

## Structure
1. **Activity Summary**: Describe the flow of funds (e.g., received $50k and wired $50k same day).
2. **Velocity Details**:
    - **Velocity Score**: Note the high velocity (e.g., > 5.0).
    - **Latency**: "Funds were held for only X mins/hours".
    - **Frequency**: "Pattern repeated daily/weekly".
3. **Transaction Details**: List the key rapid transfers (Source -> Destination).
4. **Economic Logic**: State why this speed is unusual (e.g., standard bills are monthly, wire transfers are usually for larger, infrequent needs).

## Example Phrases
- "The account exhibits extremely high velocity of funds movement, inconsistent with normal personal use."
- "Funds were credited via ACH and immediately wired out, suggesting pass-through activity."
- "The rapid dissipation of funds suggests an attempt to remove proceeds before scrutiny."
- "No business rationale identified for the immediate transfer of funds to unrelated entities."
