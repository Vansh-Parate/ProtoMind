# SAR Narrative Template — Standard Format

## 1. Subject Information
Provide identifying details of the subject(s) involved:
- Full legal name and any known aliases
- Date of birth, SSN/TIN (if available)
- Account number(s) and account type(s)
- Occupation and employer
- Relationship to the financial institution
- KYC profile summary and any discrepancies

## 2. Activity Overview
Summarize the suspicious activity:
- Date range of suspicious activity
- Total dollar amount involved
- Number and types of transactions
- Accounts and institutions involved
- Geographic locations (branches, ATMs, wire destinations)

## 3. Suspicious Activity Description
Provide a detailed, chronological narrative of the suspicious activity:
- Describe each significant transaction or pattern
- Explain WHY the activity is suspicious
- Note any attempts to evade detection
- Include relevant customer statements or explanations
- Reference specific rules or thresholds triggered

## 4. Transaction Indicators
List the specific indicators that flagged this activity:
- Automated alert triggers (threshold breaches, pattern matches)
- Manual observations by branch staff or analysts
- Third-party information (law enforcement referrals, media reports)
- Comparison to expected account behavior based on KYC profile

## 5. Typology Mapping
Classify the activity against known AML typologies:
- Primary typology (e.g., Structuring, Layering, Mule Activity)
- Secondary typologies if applicable
- Confidence level in classification

## 6. Risk Assessment
Provide overall risk evaluation:
- Risk score and methodology
- Confidence score
- Aggravating factors
- Mitigating factors

## 7. Recommendation
State the recommended action:
- File SAR with FinCEN
- Continue monitoring with enhanced due diligence
- Close alert with documented rationale
- Escalate, including recommended escalation assignment (compliance officer, law enforcement)

## Writing Guidelines
- Use clear, factual language — avoid opinions or conclusions about criminality
- Present information in chronological order
- Include specific dates, amounts, and account numbers
- Avoid jargon; write for a regulatory audience
- Each paragraph should address a single point
- Cross-reference supporting documentation where available
