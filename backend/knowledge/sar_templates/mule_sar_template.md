# SAR Template: Money Mule / Pass-Through Accounts

## SAR Narrative Structure
1. **Introduction**:
    - Identify the customer and the unusual account activity (e.g., receipt of funds followed by immediate outbound transfers).
    - Classify as potential Money Mule / Pass-Through Account activity.

2. **Activity Description**:
    - **Source of Funds**: Detail incoming transactions (ACH, Wire, Check) from potentially unrelated parties, likely victims of scams (Business Email Compromise, Romance Fraud, etc.).
    - **Speed of Movement**: Emphasize the rapid withdrawal or transfer (often same-day).
    - **Destination**: Identify where the funds went (Wire to Nigeria, P2P transfer, Gift Cards, Crypto).

3. **Behavioral Red Flags**:
    - New account opened solely for pass-through activity.
    - Customer shows confusion or non-cooperation when questioned.
    - Use of remote access tools (RATs) or unusual login locations.

4. **Risk Assessment**:
    - State clearly: "The activity is consistent with money mule behavior, facilitating the movement of illicit funds."
    - Mention negative news or known scam reports linked to originators if any.

## Key Phrases
- "Account appears to be utilized solely as a conduit for funds."
- "Customer received funds from unrelated third-parties with no logical explanation."
- "Immediate dissipation of funds suggests an intent to avoid detection or freezing."
- "Customer profile (e.g., student, unemployed) does not support high-velocity transactions."
