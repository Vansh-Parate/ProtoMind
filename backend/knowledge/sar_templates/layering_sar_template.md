# SAR Template: Layering (Typology)

## Structure
1. **Activity Summary**: Describe the layering pattern (e.g., complex transfers across multiple accounts/institutions).
2. **Layering Details**:
    - **Step 1 (Source)**: Initial deposits or transfers into the primary account.
    - **Step 2 (Movement)**: Subsequent transfers to secondary accounts or different financial institutions. Use specific dates and amounts.
    - **Step 3 (Integration/Withdrawal)**: Final disposition (e.g., wire out, investment, crypto purchase).
3. **Red Flags**:
    - High volume of transfers with no clear economic purpose.
    - Use of unrelated third-party accounts (intermediaries).
    - Rapid movement to obfuscate the origin of funds.
    - Transfers to high-risk jurisdictions or entities.
4. **Economic Logic**: State why the activity is inconsistent with the customer's known profile (e.g., "Customer is a student but moving $500k monthly").

## Example Phrases
- "Funds were layered through multiple accounts to obscure the beneficial owner."
- "The pattern of transactions suggests an attempt to distance the funds from their original source."
- "Transfers were executed in a circular manner with no apparent commercial justification."
- "Despite the high volume of activity, the account balance remained minimal, indicative of pass-through activity."
